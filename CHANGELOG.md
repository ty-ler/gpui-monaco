# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). Until 1.0, minor versions may break the API.

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed
- **Widget infrastructure (#245):** the editor now handles the wheel, clicks, clipping, paint order and focus around its zones and widgets once, for every widget.
  - **Wheel:** the wheel over an overlay widget (find, quick input, context menu), an overflowing widget (below) or a zone widget (the peek view, the problem zone) no longer scrolls the editor, as Monaco's are outside its scrollable element; scrollable widgets scroll instead. The suggest list and the quick input list scroll with the wheel, with a scrollbar on the suggest list; parameter hints get Monaco's maximum height (a quarter of the editor, at least 250px) and scroll; the hover, suggest details, code action menu, rename suggestions and peek results scroll without the editor. Plain view zones, content widgets and the gutter still scroll the editor, as in Monaco. The diff editor's gutter and moved-lines column scroll the modified editor (Monaco `delegateScrollFromMouseWheelEvent`).
  - **Clicks:** right- and middle-clicks inside widgets no longer reach the editor: it doesn't take focus (which closed the quick input, rename box, code action menu and context menu), open its context menu, start middle-click scrolling or follow a link under the widget. GPUI's click-to-focus no longer focuses the editor for clicks on its widgets either.
  - **Overflowing widgets:** `ContentWidget` and `OverlayWidget` gain `allow_editor_overflow` (Monaco `allowEditorOverflow`). Such widgets are painted above everything in the window (GPUI deferred draws, by Monaco's z-order), can extend past the editor, take the mouse from what's under them, and a content widget fits above or below its line against the window with Monaco's 22px clearance (`_layoutBoxInPage`). The hover, suggest widget, parameter hints, code action menu, context menu, read-only and other messages, rename box, post-edit widget, standalone color picker, inline suggestion toolbar and inspect tokens widget overflow, so they show in full in a short editor (a peek preview, a diff side, a one-line editor), and the hover, suggest list, parameter hints, code action menu and context menu are capped to the room in the window and flip to the side with room. The inline suggestion toolbar's "⋯" menu is a popup over everything, so the hover no longer clips it.
  - **Clipping and paint order:** zones and widgets take clicks only where they show: parts scrolled out of the editor, or under the sticky scroll and the scrollbars, no longer take clicks over the host's UI, the sticky lines or the scrollbars. Content and glyph margin widgets are clipped to the text area and the gutter and paint under the sticky scroll, as in Monaco; content widgets are kept within the text area horizontally. The long-distance inline edit hint and the gutter indicators no longer paint over the suggest list and parameter hints or take their clicks.
  - **Focus:** focusing the hover (Cmd/Ctrl+K Cmd/Ctrl+I twice) no longer closes it, so its keyboard scrolling and Escape work. The hover, suggest, parameter hints and inline suggestions now close when the focus leaves the editor and its widgets (Monaco `onDidBlurEditorWidget`), not when it moves into one of them; suggest and parameter hints used to stay open.
  - **Widget inputs:** the find, replace, quick input and rename inputs are plain inputs, as Monaco's `<input>`s: no quick or triggered suggestions, ghost text, parameter hints, hovers, inlay hints, code lenses, lightbulb, links, occurrence and selection highlights, bracket colors, unicode highlights or drag and drop. They paint `input.background` and don't follow the font zoom; the find, replace and quick inputs use the UI font at 13px, and rename keeps the editor's font, as in Monaco.
  - **Registries:** those inputs and the editor's placeholder models (the peek view's "no preview available", the multi diff editor's missing sides) are no longer in `editors()`, `models()`, `on_did_create_editor` or `on_did_create_model` (Monaco `isSimpleWidget`); they still follow global themes. The peek view's preview editor is still listed, as Monaco's embedded editors are.
  - **Pointer:** the arrow over the gutter, scrollbars, minimap and widgets, and a hand over folding controls, instead of the I-beam everywhere.
  - **Drags:** selection drags, scrollbar thumb and minimap slider drags, text drag and drop and the diff editor's sash keep following the mouse once it leaves the editor (window-level mouse listeners). Monaco's drag-select autoscroll (`TopBottomDragScrolling`, `LeftRightDragScrolling`) is ported: outside the text, the editor scrolls every frame while the mouse is held still, faster the farther away it is (Monaco's lines per second), left and right too, and the selection follows to the edge line.
  - **Tests:** GPUI tests that draw frames cover the wheel over the hover, the suggest list, the context menu and peek results, right- and middle-clicks in the quick input and context menu, focusing and scrolling the hover, suggest and parameter hints closing on blur, overflowing and flipping widgets, plain widget inputs, the registries and drags out of the editor (selection autoscroll and the scrollbar thumb), and zones clipped to the editor.
  - **Breaking:**
    - `ContentWidget` and `OverlayWidget` gain `allow_editor_overflow` (`false` keeps the old clipping).
    - Content widgets are clipped to the text area and placed within it horizontally, and paint under the sticky scroll; glyph margin widgets are clipped to the gutter.
    - Mouse targets no longer report a zone or widget where it's clipped away (scrolled out of the editor, under the sticky scroll or a scrollbar), and widgets painted above the editor take the mouse from it, so the editor's mouse events no longer fire under overflowing widgets.
    - Clicks on content and overlay widgets and zone widgets no longer focus the editor.
- **Options: validation, defaults and layout widths (#262):**
  - **Validation:** `EditorOptions::validated` ports Monaco's `validate` methods, and the editor renders with it while `CodeEditor::options` still returns the options as set (Monaco's `getRawOptions`). `font_size` is clamped to 6–100px with 0 meaning the default; `line_height` to 0–150, where `None` or 0 is automatic and a value below 8 multiplies the font size, rounded and at least 8px (`resolved_line_height`, so `Some(px(1.5))` is no longer a 1.5px line and `Some(px(0.))` no longer a zero line height the layout divided by). `fast_scroll_sensitivity` of 0 or less is 5 and `mouse_wheel_scroll_sensitivity` of 0 is 1 (0 used to stop wheel scrolling). `minimap.scale` (1–3), `minimap.max_column`, `minimap.section_header_font_size`, `sticky_scroll.max_line_count` (1–20), `folding_maximum_regions`, `color_decorators_limit`, `line_numbers_min_chars`, `code_lens_font_size`, `padding`, the scrollbar sizes, `overview_ruler_lanes`, `reveal_horizontal_right_padding`, `multi_cursor_limit`, the hover and inline suggestion delays, `font_weight`, ruler columns and the suggest and inlay hint font sizes get Monaco's limits too; whole-number options are truncated as Monaco's `clampedInt` does.
  - **Tab and indent sizes:** `ModelOptions::validated`, which `TextModel` applies on creation and in `update_options`, keeps `tab_size` and `indent_size` at least 1 (Monaco `TextModelResolvedOptions`). A tab size of 0 used to panic with a division by zero when typing or drawing a tab. The new `indent_size_is_tab_size` is Monaco's `indentSize: 'tabSize'`: the indent size follows the tab size, and models created with `detect_indentation` start with it on, as Monaco's do (`detect_indentation`, Indent Using Spaces/Tabs set a number).
  - **Layout widths:** the gutter and minimap are laid out by a port of Monaco's `EditorLayoutInfoComputer.computeLayout`, which `layout_info()` now reports as computed rather than read back. The glyph margin is a line height wide (was 18px). The folding column is Monaco's 16px, and absent with `show_folding_controls: Never` (was up to 18px whenever `folding` was on; collapsed regions' chevrons no longer show with `Never`). The minimap takes Monaco's proportional share of the width plus its 8px gutter, at most `max_column` characters (was `max_column × scale`, up to a quarter of the editor, about twice Monaco's width in narrow editors), and `minimap.size: Fill`/`Fit` widen its scale as Monaco's do, remembering it across frames while wrapping (`ComputeOptionsMemory`). Viewport wrapping uses Monaco's column count, leaving 2px for the cursor (one more column than before in most editors).
  - **`lineDecorationsWidth` in characters:** `line_decorations_width` is a `LineDecorationsWidth`, `Pixels` (from `px(…).into()`) or `Chars(n)` for Monaco's `"Nch"` strings.
  - **Occurrence highlights:** `occurrences_highlight_delay` defaults to Monaco's 0 (was 250ms). As in Monaco's `WordHighlighter`, highlights are computed once the cursor has rested 50ms and shown no sooner than the delay after it moved, so they appear about 200ms sooner.
  - **Cursor width:** `cursor_width` defaults to Monaco's 0, which draws a 2px caret (a host passing 0 used to get an invisible one), and the caret is at most a character wide.
  - **Platform defaults:** `render_final_newline` is `Dimmed` on Linux and `default_eol` CRLF on Windows, as in Monaco.
  - **Standalone options:** `stable_peek` (Monaco `stablePeek`) keeps peek views open on Escape and on double clicks in their preview. `set_auto_detect_high_contrast` (Monaco `autoDetectHighContrast`, on) switches editors to `hc-black`/`hc-light` and back as the OS's high contrast mode changes; GPUI doesn't report that mode, so hosts report it with `set_os_high_contrast`. `accessibility_help_url` is stored for when accessibility help exists. `tab_index` (Monaco `tabIndex`) sets the editor's place in the Tab order, -1 removing it.
  - **gotoLocation:** `alternative_tests_command` joins `multiple_tests` for hosts' go to test commands, and `GotoLocationOptions::with_multiple` stands in for the deprecated `multiple`, setting every `multiple_*` as VS Code's migration does.
  - **Docs:** the book's options page documents validation, the layout, global options and how option shapes differ from Monaco's (`suggest.show*` as `hidden_kinds`, `suggestFontSize`, the scrollbar sizes, `bracketPairColorizationOptions`), and which Monaco options are absent because they would do nothing.
  - **Tests:** Monaco's `editorLayoutProvider.test.ts` is ported (all 20 tests) with the wrapping tests of `editorConfiguration.test.ts`, plus unit tests for each option family's validation, the line height rules, the cursor width, the folding column, glyph margin lanes, `"Nch"` widths and the fill memory; GPUI tests cover a tab size of 0, `indentSize: 'tabSize'`, rendering with validated options, the gutter widths, the word highlighter's timing, `stablePeek` and high contrast detection.
  - **Breaking:**
    - `EditorOptions::line_decorations_width` is a `LineDecorationsWidth` instead of `Pixels` (`px(10.).into()`).
    - `EditorOptions` gains `tab_index`, `stable_peek` and `accessibility_help_url`, `ModelOptions` gains `indent_size_is_tab_size` and `GotoLocationOptions` gains `alternative_tests_command` (use `..Default::default()` in literals).
    - Defaults changed to Monaco's: `occurrences_highlight_delay` 0, `cursor_width` 0 (still drawn 2px), and on Linux `render_final_newline: Dimmed`, on Windows `default_eol: Crlf`.
    - Layout widths changed as above, so gutter, minimap and wrapping positions move.
- **Theme colors and token rules (#263):**
  - **Color registry:** theme colors now resolve as Monaco's `StandaloneTheme.getColor` does. A theme's own colors come first, then the defaults for its kind (dark, light, hcDark, hcLight) from a port of the `registerColor` calls in Monaco's `editor/**` and VS Code's `platform/theme/common/colors/*.ts` (at the vendored commit), with their `transparent`, `lighten`, `darken`, `oneOf`, `ifDefinedThenElse` and `lessProminent` expressions. The built-in themes are `themes.ts`' few colors over those defaults. A default that derives from another color follows it when a theme or `Theme::define` sets that color: `editor.selectionBackground` moves the inactive selection, fold background and minimap selection, `foreground` the widget border and menu separators, `focusBorder` the active input option, `scrollbarSlider.*` the minimap slider, `editorInfo.foreground` the peek border and banner icon, `list.activeSelection*` the menu, quick input and banner, `badge.background` inlay hints, and so on. Deprecated ids (`editorActiveLineNumber.foreground`) are defaults too.
  - **Stale defaults:** vs `editor.selectionHighlightBackground` is `#ADD6FF4D`; `editorWidget.border` and `menu.separatorBackground` are the foreground at 20% (`#CCCCCC33` / `#61616133`); `editorInfo.foreground` is `#59A4F9` / `#0063D3`, which the peek border, banner icon and info markers use; vs `peekViewTitleDescription.foreground` is `#616161` (it was a translucent teal); vs code lens `#919191`, snippet tab stops `#0A326433`, active input option `#0090F133`, minimap slider half the scrollbar's, find range `#B4B4B44D`; hc-light `multiDiffEditor.border` `#CCCCCC`; light inline edit indicator background 50% and a darkened modified border; `editor.compositionBorder` (`ime_underline`) white or black.
  - **Markers:** squiggles, overview ruler marks and the marker navigation zone take the theme's `editorError/Warning/Info/Hint.*`, `editorOverviewRuler.{error,warning,info}Foreground` and `editorMarkerNavigation*` colors instead of fixed dark-theme ones, so light themes get Monaco's light squiggles. `DecorationOptions::severity` marks a marker's decorations for this, and infos are marked in the overview ruler too, as in Monaco. `editor*.background` fills marker ranges when a theme sets it.
  - **Widgets:** the context menu uses `menu.*` (Monaco's dropdown colors, `menu.separatorBackground` separators, a `menu.selectionBorder` outline in high contrast); hovers and parameter hints `editorHoverWidget.*` (the active parameter in `highlightForeground`, the status bar in `statusBarBackground`); the code action list `editorActionList.*`; rename candidates `list.activeSelection*`; quick access `quickInput.*`, `quickInputTitle.background`, `quickInputList.*`, `list.highlightForeground`, `pickerGroup.*` and `keybindingLabel.*` keybindings; find toggles `inputOption.hoverBackground` and `activeForeground`, the invalid regex border `inputValidation.errorBorder`; toolbar buttons (find, hover copy, marker zone) `toolbar.hoverBackground` / `hoverOutline`; the suggest widget `selectedIconForeground`, `focusOutline` and `editorSuggestWidgetStatus.foreground`.
  - **High contrast:** find matches, find scopes, word, selection, range and symbol highlights, peek matches and code action highlights are outlined with `editor.findMatchBorder`, `editor.wordHighlightBorder` and the other border keys instead of the translucent fills that approximated them; focused list rows get `list.focusOutline`.
  - **Editor surfaces:** indent guides by level (`editorIndentGuide.background1`–`6`, cycling over the visible ones), bracket pair guides (`editorBracketPairGuide.*`, else the bracket color), `editor.inactiveLineHighlightBackground` when unfocused (and a 1px current line border in high contrast), the minimap slider's hover and active colors, `editorOverviewRuler.background` and `rangeHighlightForeground`, `editor.foldPlaceholderForeground`, `editorGutter.foldingControlForeground`, `editorStickyScroll.shadow`, `.border` and `editorStickyScrollGutter.background`, the peek editor's gutter and sticky scroll, `editor.wordHighlightText*` for textual occurrences and `diffEditorOverview.*` are drawn. The current line border follows Monaco: dropped when a theme sets a visible `editor.lineHighlightBackground` without it.
  - **New keys:** `EditorColors` gains a field for each of these and for cursor (`editorCursor.background`, `editorMultiCursor.*`), `editorLineNumber.dimmedForeground`, `editorBracketMatch.foreground`, `editorLightBulbAi.foreground`, `editor.findMatch*Foreground`, `editor.hoverHighlightBackground`, minimap highlights, `widget.shadow`, `widget.border`, `editorWidget.resizeBorder`, `input.*`, `inputValidation.*`, `button.*`, `checkbox.*`, `icon.foreground`, `descriptionForeground`, `disabledForeground`, `contrastBorder`, `contrastActiveBorder` and `inlineEdit.*` (`gutterIndicator.background`, `tabWillAccept*Border`). All of them are read from VS Code theme JSON, which also now maps `editorCodeLens.foreground`, `editor.linkedEditingBackground`, `editor.rangeHighlightBackground`, `diffEditor.move*.border` and `multiDiffEditor.*`.
  - **VS Code themes:** a theme's unset colors get the registry defaults for its `type`, so `hcDark`/`hc` and `hcLight` themes start from the high contrast defaults rather than `vs-dark`'s and `vs`'s.
  - **Token rules:** rules resolve as Monaco's `TokenTheme` trie: a rule inherits the color or font style it leaves unset from its parent (a `fontStyle`-only `comment.doc` keeps `comment`'s color, vs-dark's `metatag.php` is bold in `metatag`'s color), equal selectors merge, `fontStyle: ''` clears an inherited style, and `''` rules (and `tokenColors` entries without a scope) set the default. VS Code `tokenColors` and semantic token fallbacks resolve the same way. `fontStyle` is split on spaces into whole words, and Monaco rule colors must be `RRGGBB`/`RRGGBBAA` (alpha ignored).
  - **Language-specific rules:** a tree-sitter capture's Monaco token carries its language, as Monaco's tokens do, so `keyword.json`, `delimiter.html`, `attribute.value.html`, `string.yaml` and other language rules apply: JSON `true`/`false`/`null` are `#CE9178` in vs-dark and `#0451A5` in vs, JSON string values `string.value.json`, HTML punctuation `#383838` in vs. The editor, `colorize`, `colorize_model_line` and "Inspect Tokens" use the language of the injection a span is in. `Theme::style_for_language` and `monaco_token_for_capture_in` expose it; `monaco_token_type` no longer doubles the suffix of `string.key.json`. The built-in themes now include all of Monaco's language rules (`*.pug`, `*.scss`, `metatag.*`, `attribute.value.*`, `string.html/sql/yaml`, `operator.*`, `predefined.sql`) and its `''` default rule.
  - **`Theme::define`:** with `inherit: false`, only the new colors apply over the registry defaults for the base's kind (Monaco drops the base's `themes.ts` colors), and with `inherit` a custom base keeps its colors except those the new keys change. `editor.foreground` becomes a `''` token rule, as in Monaco.
  - **Semantic tokens:** with a standalone theme (one with Monaco rules), a semantic token no rule matches gets the default token style, overriding the syntax color, as Monaco's `getTokenStyleMetadata` does.
  - **Tests:** Monaco's `tokenization.test.ts` is ported (deeper matches, matching, parsing, defaults, merging, inheritance); unit tests cover the registry's transforms and references, the defaults per built-in theme for the changed keys, markers, menus, lists and hovers, VS Code JSON mapping and high contrast types, derived defaults through `Theme::define`, `inherit: false`, custom bases, language-specific rules, rule inheritance and `''` rules, color and font style parsing, and the semantic token fallback; GPUI tests cover high contrast find outlines, marker severities and theme colors, and JSON colorizing.
  - **Breaking:**
    - `EditorColors` gains many fields (build it with `EditorColors::vs_dark()` and the like, or `Theme::define`, rather than a literal), and `indent_guide` / `indent_guide_active` are replaced by `indent_guides` / `indent_guides_active` (`[Option<Hsla>; 6]`) and `indent_guide_colors`. Its defaults changed as above.
    - `InlineEditColors` fields are resolved colors instead of `Option`s, and the unused `inline_edit_original_background`, `inline_edit_original_changed_line_background` and `inline_edit_modified_changed_line_background` methods are gone (read the fields).
    - `TokenRule`'s `style: TokenStyle` is replaced by `foreground: Option<Hsla>` and `font_style: Option<TokenFontStyle>`, where `None` inherits; `TokenRule::monaco` builds one from Monaco's strings.
    - `DecorationOptions` gains `severity` (use `..Default::default()` in literals).
    - The built-in themes' default rule gives unmatched tokens the editor foreground explicitly (`style_for` returns `Some` rather than `None`).
    - `MarkerSeverity::color` returns vs-dark's `#59A4F9` for infos and a translucent `#EEEEEE` for hints.
- **Languages API: provider ranking, registration and types (#265):**
  - **Provider ranking:** every provider lookup now ranks providers as Monaco's `LanguageFeatureRegistry.ordered` does: by selector score (an exact language, scheme or path 10, `*` 5), then the most recently registered first. They used to run in registration order, so an older `*` or `LanguageSelector::All` provider beat a newer or language-specific one for rename, formatting ("the first one registered is used"), on-type formatting, signature help, document highlights and linked editing, and hover sections came out in registration order. Links and inlay hints are asked worst first, as Monaco's `ordered(model).reverse()`, so the best-ranked links win; semantic tokens and drop and paste providers no longer reverse the list themselves.
  - **Selectors:** `LanguageSelector::score` and the free `providers::score` (Monaco `languages.score`) are public, as is `LanguageFilter::score`. `*` in `LanguageSelector::Languages` matches any language (score 5, as `All` now scores), empty filter fields count as unset, and `LanguageFilter::exclusive` (Monaco `exclusive`) makes a matching provider the only one of its kind. `hasAccessToAllModels` and `notebookType` don't apply.
  - **Completion groups:** the suggest widget asks the providers in groups of equal score (`completion_provider_groups`, Monaco `orderedGroups`) and stops at the first group with items, so a `*` provider is skipped when the language's own providers answer (Monaco `provideSuggestionItems`). Ghost-text suggestions (`quick_suggestions` `Inline`) do the same.
  - **Trigger characters and incomplete lists:** a trigger character asks only the providers that declare it, and keeps the open session's items from the others (Monaco `providerFilter` and `providerItemsToReuse`); it doesn't trigger when it's a word character starting quick suggestions (`checkTriggerCharacter`). Typing on after an incomplete list asks only the providers whose lists were incomplete and keeps the others' items, and starting a new word while the widget shows keeps every provider's items and asks only providers that had none. Kept items keep the ranges they were given (or the session's default ones).
  - **Registering a language again:** `LanguageRegistry::register` with an existing id merges into it, as Monaco's `LanguagesRegistry._mergeLanguage`: extensions, file names, patterns, aliases and MIME types are added; the name, first-line pattern, grammar and highlighter are replaced only when the new registration has them; and configuration fields it sets replace the ones it was registered with. It used to replace the language, dropping its grammar, configuration and any Monarch highlighter.
  - **Language configurations:** `set_language_configuration` layers a configuration over the language's, as Monaco's `LanguageConfigurationRegistry`: each field from the newest configuration that sets it, so `{ comments }` alone keeps the brackets and auto-closing pairs. It returns a `Disposable` that removes it, restoring what it replaced. `LanguageConfiguration::combine` is Monaco's `combineLanguageConfigurations`, and `LanguageRegistry::register_configuration`/`unregister_configuration` keep the stack. An explicit `"autoClosingPairs": []` now turns auto-closing off instead of falling back to the brackets, as in Monaco. Monaco's deprecated `__electricCharacterSupport.docComment` is parsed (`LanguageConfiguration::electric_character_support`) and auto-closed like an auto-closing pair, as Monaco's `CharacterPairSupport` does.
  - **Location links:** `Location::origin_selection_range` and `target_selection_range` (Monaco `LocationLink`) for definition, declaration, type definition, implementation and reference results: Cmd/Ctrl+hover underlines the origin range (grown to cover every result's), navigating selects and highlights the target selection range, and peek uses it as Monaco's `OneReference.range`, while the definition hover shows the whole `range`. `Location::new` and `selection_range` help build and read them. The LSP crate keeps `LocationLink`s' ranges; it used to use only the target selection range.
  - **Workspace edits:** `WorkspaceEdit` gains `file_edits` (`WorkspaceFileEdit` with `WorkspaceFileEditOptions`, Monaco `IWorkspaceFileEdit`), `snippet_edits` (`WorkspaceSnippetEdit`, Monaco's `insertAsSnippet` text edits), `versions` (Monaco `versionId`) and `metadata` (`WorkspaceEditMetadata`, Monaco `needsConfirmation` and `label`). Applying one checks the versions first ("bad state - model changed in the meantime", as standalone Monaco), sends the file operations to the host's `set_workspace_file_edit_handler` (standalone Monaco rejects them; without a handler the edit fails), then edits the text; a single snippet edit in the editor starts a snippet session with the other edits as one undo step, and the others insert their plain text. Edits that need confirmation go through the preview handler, as VS Code's bulk edit service does. `CodeEditor::apply_workspace_edit` is now asynchronous. The LSP crate converts file operations and change annotations (`lsp-types` reads annotated text edits as plain ones, so only file operations' annotations and edits it parses as annotated carry metadata).
  - **Tokens providers:** `set_tokens_provider` (Monaco `languages.setTokensProvider`) highlights a language with a line-state `syntax::TokensProvider` (`initial_state`, `tokenize(line, state)` returning `LineTokens` of `Token`s, Monaco `TokensProvider`), run by `syntax::TokensProviderHighlighter`, which re-tokenizes edits until a line ends in the state it had before. `register_tokens_provider_factory` (Monaco `registerTokensProviderFactory`) creates a `LanguageTokenizer` (a highlighter or a Monarch grammar) when the language is first needed, for Monaco's `Thenable` providers too. Both, and `set_monarch_tokens_provider`, return a `Disposable` restoring the previous highlighting. `EncodedTokensProvider` has no equivalent, like `setColorMap`.
  - **Language events:** `on_language_encountered` (Monaco `onLanguageEncountered`) runs once when a language is first needed for basic features: when a model uses it, or when a Monarch grammar's `nextEmbedded` meets it while tokenizing (`LanguageRegistry::note_language_encountered`), which is where to set an embedded language's tokenizer lazily. `on_language` returns a `Disposable` that cancels the callback (Monaco `onLanguage`).
  - **Monarch before registration:** `set_monarch_tokens_provider` no longer needs the language registered, as in Monaco: the grammar applies once it is (`LanguageRegistry::set_highlighter` keeps highlighters for unregistered ids, `pending_highlighter` reads them).
  - **Rename:** rename locations and edits are asked for one provider at a time, best first, stopping at the first answer (Monaco `RenameSkeleton`), instead of starting every provider up front.
  - **Provider fields:** `Command::tooltip` (shown on hover over code lenses) and `Command::new`; `CodeAction::is_ai` and `ranges`; `CodeActionProvider::display_name` and `extension_id`; `DocumentSymbol::container_name` (the LSP crate fills it for flat symbols); `DocumentSymbolProvider::display_name`; `CodeLens::id`; `InlayHintsProvider::display_name`; `CompletionItem::action` (run by ghost-text suggestions without a `command`). Monaco's internal `extensionId`, `CompletionList.duration` and `FoldingRangeProvider.id` aren't ported.
  - **Drop and paste:** `DocumentDropEditProvider::id`, `provided_drop_edit_kinds` and `resolve_document_drop_edit`; `DocumentPasteEditProvider::id`, `resolve_document_paste_edit` and `provide_document_paste_edits_with_context` with a `DocumentPasteContext` (Monaco's, with `only`, the kind "Paste As..." asks for); `DocumentDropOrPasteEdit::additional_edit` (Monaco `additionalEdit`, a `WorkspaceEdit` applied after the edit). The edit being applied is resolved first, as Monaco's `PostEditWidgetManager` does.
  - **Tests:** Monaco's `languageSelector.test.ts` scores are ported, and `suggestModel.test.ts`'s "#21484: Trigger character always force a new completion session", "registerCompletionItemProvider with letters as trigger characters block other completion items to show up #127815", "Snippets gone from IntelliSense #173244" (incomplete re-queries) and "Trigger (full) completions when (incomplete) completions are already active #99504"; GPUI tests cover provider ranking (score, newest, `*`, exclusive), completion groups, the best-ranked formatter, location links (selection, highlight, underline, hover), lazy rename resolution, workspace file operations, versions, snippet edits and confirmation, configuration layering and disposing, merged registration, `set_tokens_provider`, tokens provider factories, `on_language_encountered` for models and embedded languages, cancelling `on_language`, Monarch grammars set before registration, paste edit resolution and `only`, and code lens tooltips; unit tests cover the tokens provider highlighter (states across lines, incremental re-tokenizing, token order), configuration combining and parsing, registry merging and configuration stacks.
  - **Breaking:**
    - Providers are ranked by score, then newest first, instead of registration order: where several match, a different one may now win.
    - `LanguageConfiguration`'s `comments`, `brackets`, `auto_closing_pairs`, `surrounding_pairs` and `on_enter_rules` are `Option`s (wrap literals in `Some`; read them with the `comments()`, `brackets()`, `auto_closing_pairs()`, `surrounding_pairs()` and `on_enter_rules()` accessors), and it gains `electric_character_support`.
    - `set_language_configuration` returns `anyhow::Result<Disposable>` instead of `bool`, and merges instead of replacing; `LanguageRegistry::set_configuration` is replaced by `register_configuration` and `unregister_configuration`.
    - `LanguageRegistry::register` merges into an existing language instead of replacing it.
    - `on_language` returns a `Disposable`.
    - `set_monarch_tokens_provider` succeeds for languages that aren't registered yet.

  - They translate more JavaScript syntax: `[^]`, `[]`, a literal `[` in a class, and Annex B hyphens such as `[\w-.]`.
  - They ignore `g`, `y`, `d`, `u` and `v` flags.
  - `Pattern::error` and `LanguageConfiguration::patterns` report regexes that don't compile.
  - The bundled HTML `onEnterRules` and CSS `wordPattern` never compiled before and now work.

Also fixed:

- **Undo:** `text::Buffer::set_last_transaction_metadata` sets the cursor state of the open transaction, if there is one, instead of the previous undo step.
- **View zones:** many zones (for example thousands of code lenses) no longer slow down every frame. Zone positions are found by binary search, only zones on screen are rendered, and zone anchors are re-based as they're resolved.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-09

This is the first release: a Monaco-like code editor component for GPUI. It is distributed through git and pinned to gpui at zed rev `f16f9652ec57bf806e65b2a0d51bb92a63644914`. See the [guide](book/src/getting-started.md) for setup.

### Text (`gpui-monaco-text`)
- **Rope:** built on `sum_tree`, with byte, char, UTF-16 and line summaries, and `Point` / `PointUtf16` / `OffsetUtf16` coordinates.
- **Edits:** batch edits, with `Patch` composition, inversion and offset transforms.
- **Anchors:** resolved lazily with left or right bias.
- **Undo:** operation-based undo and redo with edit grouping and transactions.
- **Line endings:** LF and CRLF are detected and normalized, and a BOM is preserved.
- **Conformance:** Monaco's piece-tree and text-model buffer tests are ported.

### Syntax (`gpui-monaco-syntax`, `gpui-monaco-languages`)
- **`LanguageRegistry`:** finds languages by id, alias, extension and file name.
- **Editing rules:** VS Code `language-configuration.json` parsing, with `fancy-regex` patterns.
- **Highlighting:** tree-sitter, with incremental reparsing on a background thread and injections, including combined injections and `include-children`. Unchanged injection layers and their query results are reused across reparses.
- **Custom highlighters:** a pluggable `Highlighter` trait, set per language with `Language::with_highlighter`.
- **Bundled languages:** each is behind a cargo feature: Rust, JavaScript/JSX, TypeScript/TSX, JSON, YAML, TOML, Markdown (with fenced-code injections), HTML (with `<script>` and `<style>`) and CSS.

### Editor (`gpui-monaco`)
- **Model and view:**
  - A `TextModel` entity holds the text, language, undo history, decorations and markers.
  - Any number of `CodeEditor` views can share a model. Edits move the other views' cursors, and undo restores the cursors of the view that made the edit.
  - View state can be saved and restored.
- **Cursor core:** ported from Monaco's `cursor` with its unit tests:
  - character, word, word-part, line and page movement
  - column selection and multi-cursor
  - typing, deleting, tab and outdent
  - line operations
  - clipboard with multi-cursor distribution
  - IME composition
- **Typing helpers:** Monaco's rules for auto-closing pairs, overtype, auto-surround, auto-indent (`onEnterRules`, `indentationRules`, electric characters) and bracket matching.
- **Find and replace:** a find widget with case, whole-word, regex (Rust `regex`) and in-selection search, plus replace and replace all with `$n` substitution.
- **Multi-cursor commands:** add the next match, move to the next match, select all occurrences, and add cursors above or below.
- **View:**
  - gutter with line numbers, glyph margin and fold controls
  - current-line highlight
  - scrollbars with an overview ruler
  - word wrap following Monaco's line-break rules
  - indentation and marker folding
  - minimap
  - sticky scroll
- **Decorations:** `delta_decorations` supports backgrounds, borders, underlines, text color, whole-line decorations, glyphs, line-number color, gutter bars, overview ruler marks, injected before/after text, hover messages, stickiness and per-editor ownership.
- **Widgets:** view zones, content widgets and overlay widgets, any of which can be a GPUI view.
- **Providers:**
  - **Completion:** Monaco's `fuzzyScore` and completion model, and a suggest widget with docs.
  - **Hover:** markdown, with highlighted code blocks.
  - **Go to definition:** Cmd/Ctrl+hover link, Cmd/Ctrl+click and F12.
  - **Signature help.**
- **Diagnostics:** `set_markers` with squiggles, overview ruler marks, hover messages, and F8 navigation in an inline zone.
- **Themes:**
  - Monaco `vs` and `vs-dark`.
  - VS Code color theme JSON, with `include`; the type is inferred when the file omits it.
  - `Theme::define`.
- **Keymap:** Monaco's default keybindings per platform, as GPUI actions in the `CodeEditor` key context.

### Tooling
- **Playground:** an example app with:
  - file open and language and theme switching
  - option toggles and a status bar
  - demo providers and widgets
  - screenshot and perf modes
- **Performance:**
  - Criterion benchmarks for the text and editor components.
  - A large-file budget test: open in under 200ms, keystroke-to-paint p95 under 8ms and reparse in under 50ms on 100k lines.
  - [`docs/PERFORMANCE.md`](docs/PERFORMANCE.md) has the numbers.
- **Docs:** an mdBook guide in `book/`, and rustdoc on every public item.
